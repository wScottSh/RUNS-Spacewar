Spacewar! Verification · Explainer 0001

The Oracle establishes ground truth.
So why can't it anchor the Realization?

“I don't understand the separation between the Oracle establishing ground truth, but not being able to be used as an anchor for the Realization.”

The confusion is real and it's worth having, because the two things sound identical and aren't. The short version: “establishing ground truth” and “anchoring a Realization” are two different jobs, and the Oracle currently does the first job completely and the second job only for the math. Let's take it apart.

01Two jobs that wear the same coat

A test suite around the original can do two very different things. They feel like one thing. Naming them apart is the whole lesson.

Job A

Interrogate the original

“Did I probe every behavior the original has? Did I leave a branch un-asked?”

A statement about: Ground Truth. Measured by branch coverage of the PDP-1 assembly.
Job B

Constrain the Realization

“If my new code is wrong, will a test go red?”

A statement about: the Realization. Measured by portable, value-level assertions it can be graded against.

“Establishing ground truth” is Job A. And you nailed it — 640 tests green, 20 seconds of real SIMH per gate run, every in-contract branch of the original driven both ways. That genuinely establishes what the original does.

“Anchoring the Realization” is Job B. And here is the trap: the metric that proves Job A is not measurable for Job B at all.

02An anchor needs two ends

Think of a single test-assertion as an anchor chain. To hold the boat, it must be fastened at both ends: one end sunk into the seabed (Ground Truth), the other end shackled to the boat (the Realization).

Why coverage can't reach the boat. “100% branch coverage” means every branch of the PDP-1 assembly was driven both ways. That is a fact about the original binary's control-flow graph. Your Realization is written from scratch — it has its own branches, its own control flow, no PDP-1 program counter. The question “did the Realization cover every branch of the original?” isn't hard to answer — it's not even well-formed. There is nothing on the boat to shackle that chain to.

03The exam and the answer key

Ground Truth is a professor who knows Spacewar! perfectly.

Job A — interrogation. You quiz the professor and check off: “I asked a question about every chapter — sqrt, sin, gravity, collision, hyperspace, explosion. Nothing skipped.” That checklist is branch coverage. It proves your questioning was thorough. It is a property of how you interrogated the professor.

Job B — grading a student. Now a student (the Realization) sits the same exam. To grade them you need the professor's written answers — an answer key in a form the student's paper can be checked against.

And here's the split that's confusing you:

  • Math chapters: you wrote the answers down. 262,144 sine answers, 262,144 cosine, 65,536 square roots — the full domain, frozen in *-vectors.jsonl. A real answer key. Grade any student, bit-for-bit.
  • Game chapters: you confirmed the professor answered every game question (coverage) — but you watched him answer aloud in his native tongue (PDP-1 core addresses, PC values) and never transcribed the answers into a portable key. reference/ is empty. There's nothing to grade a student against.

So “the Oracle establishes ground truth” is true — the professor demonstrably knows everything. “The Oracle anchors the Realization” requires the answer key, and that only exists for the math.

The one-line diagnosis. The Oracle has both anchor-ends for the pure-math island and only the seabed end for the game — which is the actual point of Spacewar. Its proudest artifact, the branch-coverage gate, is by nature a seabed-only anchor.

05What “anchoring the game” would actually require

Two missing pieces turn the game half from Job A into Job B:

1 · A portable observable schema

Re-express what a Trace asserts from PDP-1 core addresses (m03476, PC=01703) into game-state the Realization also has: ship position, velocity, heading, fuel, torpedo lifecycle. That's the shackle for the boat-end. (ADR-0012:91 already calls the per-frame snapshot “the truth” — it just isn't frozen in portable form yet.)

2 · A differential harness, not a frozen file

The strongest answer key is the professor himself. Run new code and live SIMH on the same expanding, randomized inputs and diff their observable frames. A frozen snapshot is only a lower bound (passing code can still diverge on untested inputs); the live original is the real oracle.

Bonus, and this one bites: swap the completeness bar from “branch coverage” to mutation score against SIMH. Inject a fault into the original; if no test goes red, your assertions are too weak — regardless of coverage. That's the difference between “a branch ran” and “the value it produced is pinned.”

06Check yourself

Q. You finish the game Realization. You run the coverage-union gate against it and it reports 100% branch coverage. What have you actually learned about your Realization?
