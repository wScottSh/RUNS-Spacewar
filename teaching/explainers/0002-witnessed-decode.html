01The one thing your memory got slightly wrong

You said the Oracle “already litigated this.” Half true — and the half that's off is the whole confusion. Split the surface in two:

CAPTURE  what to record, how strong, raw words, provenance, coverage-as-branch-completeness.  →  litigated to death. ADR-0004/0006/0007/0008/0012.

DECODE  how a Realization is compared against those raw words.  →  not litigated. Consciously deferred.

And this isn't interpretation — it's written into your own freeze commit, oracle/reference-snapshots.js:13:

“The words are frozen RAW … A Realization-facing decode is a phase-2 interface decision (ADR-0007 defers it to the variant author).”

So the “am I crazy?” feeling has one clean source: you remembered “we settled the capture” as “we settled everything.” You didn't. You settled ~95% of the surface and deliberately punted one square — with your own fingerprints on the punt. Against a fully-settled backdrop, one open square looks like an anomaly that must mean you're missing something. It isn't. It's punted, on purpose.

02A frozen raw word can still be reference-internal

Here's the trap the freeze sets. It feels like freezing the words gave you the boat-end shackle from 0001. For most columns, it did. For a few, it did not — and those few are exactly the game.

Open your own golden — oracle/reference/gravity.snapshot.json, the first boot frame. Look at address 3476:

"3476": "002310", // mtb — ship 1 calc-routine column
"3477": "002314", // mtb — ship 2

Now the question that cracks it open: what is 002310?

It is not a game fact. It's a PDP-1 core address — the memory location of the normal ship-calc routine. And the tell is in Ground Truth itself, at spacewar3.1_complete.txt:889:

lac (mex 400000   / yes, EXPLODE

When a ship explodes, the main loop rewrites that word to hold the address of mex, the explosion routine (:950). That's the self-modification T-MAINLOOP / EPIC #5 is about. So sit with what a single 18-bit word is carrying:

This is the irreducible idea. A frozen raw word looks like a portable, two-ended anchor. But if the value is a PDP-1 core address, the boat-end has nothing to shackle to — a from-scratch Realization has no mex, no 002310, no core map. Reference-internal and portable aren't a property of whether you froze the word — they're a property of what the word means. Freezing didn't decide it. It just deferred it.

03Why you can't have the Zelda hash

You've probably wished for the decomp trick: sha1(built.z64) == sha1(original.z64) — one mechanical hash, done. This word is exactly why you can't.

Zelda recompiles to the same substrate. Your Realization doesn't.

The N64 decomp emits N64 machine code, so a byte-identical binary is a real, achievable object — the hash has a referent. Your Exact Realization is a from-scratch DIGS expression on a different substrate. It reproduces the fact “ship is normal / exploding,” but the number 002310 has no referent over there.

So the equivalent of the matching hash isn't “same core image” — that phrase is meaningless across substrates. It's an exact match of a decoded game-state fingerprint per frame: numeric fields raw, artifact fields decoded to game meaning, pure-machine-timing fields projected out. And notice what kind of problem that is — classification, not construction. The hard part is deciding which column is which. Once classified, the projection is ~50 lines.

04The decode feels like heresy — here's the exorcism

This is the part that actually spooked you, even unnamed. Your creed is carved into CONTEXT.md: “nothing asserted, everything checked; the Translator is never authority.” The example dialogue ends: “What you never do is promote a reading of the Translator to a green test. Ever.”

And a decode layer smells exactly like that forbidden move. “This word means exploding” sounds like an interpretation being promoted to green. The prior grill voiced the fear precisely: “the adapter becomes a new trust surface — who verifies the adapter?” That whiff of heresy is what turned a trivial projection into a felt architectural hazard.

The fear

“If I write mtb == addr(mex) ⇒ exploding, I've asserted a meaning. That's Translator-class interpretation wearing a green checkmark. Heresy.”

The dissolution

Where does that map come from? Not the masswerk Translator's prose. You observe it from the Substrate — the T-MAINLOOP trace literally shows the explode branch writing mex's address into that slot, the same way you observe every raw word you already trust. It's read off Ground-Truth execution, and it's falsifiable by re-running the trace.

The line that defuses it: an interpretation read from prose is heresy; a mapping read from execution is just another observation. A witnessed decode is observe-don't-assert applied one layer up — not a violation of the creed, an instance of it. The decode map earns its green the way everything else does: it's witnessed, and it's re-runnable.

05So: three bins, not one frozen blob

The freeze treats every captured word identically — raw octal. The contract doesn't. Each column falls into one of three bins, and which bin is a witnessed classification, not a guess:

Bin A

Raw

Numeric game state. The Exact Realization is bit-exact fixed-point by axiom, so the raw word already is a two-ended portable anchor. Decoding here would only manufacture a Variant — strictly worse.

nx1 ny1 ndx ndy nom nth nfu ntr … + scalars
Bin B

Decode

The value is a PDP-1 code/memory address. Reference-internal as raw. A witnessed decode to game meaning gives it the boat-end.

mtb (calc ptr) · not (outline ptr)
Bin C — open

Drop?

Zero game-state content — pure machine timing. Candidate: nb1, the instruction-budget count (:677). No boat-end because there's no game fact to shackle.

nb1 (mtc budget) — unratified
What you already locked before you called timeout (now live in CONTEXT.md): Exact Realization (bit-exact subject of the goldens) and Variant (any non-exact Realization, not graded). Bin A is settled by axiom; Bin B is settled in principle (the mtb/Zelda call); Bin C — and whether “same” is exact-no-epsilon — is the only thing still genuinely open, and it's a decision, not a fog.

06The thesis, in one breath

It was never hard to implement. It was hard to name, and it carried a false whiff of heresy. Once you see that (a) the object table welds game-fact to machine-encoding in single words like 3476, and (b) a witnessed decode is not an asserted interpretation — the whole thing collapses back to the ~50-line projection you intuited it should be.

Your intuition was right about the code and got ambushed by a classification problem wearing a heresy costume. That's the 20%. It's real, it's small, and now it's named.
